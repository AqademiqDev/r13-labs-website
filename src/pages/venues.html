Prism does not play music. It generates it, in your room.

There's no catalogue and no stream. A device in your venue composes original audio continuously and shapes it across your trading day. You set where the room should sit — it holds it there, and moves it when you want it moved.

And it never repeats, so nobody gets tired of it. Staff fatigue is the most common reason a venue quietly stops using a system it's still paying for. Prism has no rotation to learn.

Every sound is original and owned by R13 Labs, so there's no repertoire to clear and no collecting society to pay. It runs on the device, so a slow connection never stops the music.
