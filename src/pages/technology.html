Target difference

The engine holds a target state and works on the gap, per dimension. Default target is a neutral baseline.

Actuation authority

Each dimension is only allowed to push as hard as its own confidence permits. Low confidence, small hand on the wheel.

Confidence is a weighted fusion of how well each contributing signal was actually covered in the last window. It degrades — it doesn't fall over.

Three things that make it ours.

01

It has somewhere to go

Most adaptive systems mirror the state they detect. Prism holds a target, measures the gap, and works to close it over time.

02

Uncertainty is a control input

Confidence doesn't just annotate the read — it caps how hard each dimension may act, so a shaky estimate can't produce a strong intervention.

03

The read is a clean seam

Sensing and generation only meet at the state vector. Swap the surface or the modality, keep the engine — which is why a phone, a room and a cabin share one core.

Runs where it reads

The whole loop executes locally, on the device doing the sensing. No round trip, no dependency on a connection, and no raw signal leaving the room it came from.
