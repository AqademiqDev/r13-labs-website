The Prism Engine is the proprietary technology behind everything we build.

What makes the Prism Engine

Taking signals from the device it runs on, the Prism Engine forms a live read of your state — then generates the sound to meet it, in real time, on device.

01 — INPUTS

Inputs: you, in the moment

The engine uses what your device can honestly offer. It doesn't need a wearable, and it doesn't depend on any single sensor.

02 — THE READ

A live estimate, held honestly

The engine holds a continuous estimate of your state, and of how confident it is in that estimate. Low confidence changes how it behaves.

03 — SOUND GENERATION

Sound that reshapes itself

Sound elements are designed by our audio team. Driven by the read, what you're hearing reshapes itself as your state changes.

04 — OUTPUT

Output: your device

Generated locally, in real time. Nothing has to leave your device for the sound to happen.

One engine, many surfaces

The part that reads state and the part that makes sound are separate. That's why the same engine serves a student, a room, and a cabin — and why it can drive things other than audio.
